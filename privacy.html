Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version

Speakaboo helps you learn languages by talking. We keep things small: no ads, no selling your data.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, hearts, words you starred and settings are saved on your device. If you create an account, your progress is also backed up to your account so you can use it on another device.

What we collect if you make an account

Your email address and your learning progress. Guests can use the app without an email.

Microphone

When you tap the mic to practise speaking, your phone's speech recognition (provided by your browser or phone, for example Google or Apple) listens to you to check your pronunciation. Speakaboo does not record or keep your voice. The optional "Record me" button saves a short clip only on your own device.

Translations

When you tap a word that you have not learnt yet, or choose "My language", that word or sentence is sent to a free online translation service (MyMemory) to get its meaning. No name or email is sent with it.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Children

Speakaboo is for everyone, but children under 13 should use it with a parent or guardian.

Delete your data

You can delete your account and data inside the app (Me → Account → Delete account), or write to us at the address below.
